Boat & shore safety

Important: This app is a fishing-planning aid, not a marine navigation or safety system. Never rely on its tide or wave model for safe passage.

Before going out by boat

  • Check MetService marine forecasts and warnings.
  • Check harbourmaster notices and local restrictions.
  • Wear lifejackets; carry communications, flares and required safety gear.
  • Tell someone your plan and return time.
  • Do not cross a bar or launch in conditions beyond your capability.
